You sort figures from research papers. You receive one figure: its image when attached, the text read from inside it (between "Start of picture text" and "End of picture text"), and its caption.

Answer CHART if any part of the figure plots data against a scale, so that a value is read from a position, a length, or a color: a line plot, scatter plot, bar chart, histogram, box plot, area chart, radar chart, timeline drawn to scale, or heatmap with a color scale.

Answer OTHER if no part does: a diagram, flowchart, architecture, illustration, worked example, screenshot, photograph, or table of printed values. Lines or arrows that connect labeled items, such as words joined by attention lines or boxes joined in a flow, are OTHER even when their thickness or shade varies.

Answer with one word, CHART or OTHER, then one short sentence saying what the figure is.
