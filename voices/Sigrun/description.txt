A mature female voice with a rich, resonant lower register and a controlled, confident delivery. She has a dark, velvety vocal texture, precise articulation, and a measured pace that gives each sentence weight without becoming slow or theatrical. She can sound commanding during danger, intimate in reflective passages, and subtly mysterious when the story enters uncertain territory. Emotional changes should remain restrained but clearly audible through timing, emphasis, and tonal variation rather than exaggerated acting. Well suited to literary fantasy, historical fiction, psychological drama, mythology, political intrigue, and stories led by complex adult women.
