A warm adult male voice with a textured baritone, relaxed confidence, and the instinctive timing of an experienced storyteller. He sounds inviting and deeply human, using subtle humor, expressive phrasing, and natural pauses to build atmosphere and anticipation without dramatic exaggeration. He gives dialogue distinct personality through rhythm and emphasis rather than cartoonish character voices. Reflective passages should feel intimate and thoughtful, while action and suspense gain momentum through tighter pacing and stronger vocal focus. Well suited to literary fiction, folklore, adventure, historical novels, humorous writing, family sagas, and stories intended to feel told rather than merely read.
