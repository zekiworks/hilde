A charismatic adult female voice with a luminous, full-bodied tone and a confident natural rhythm. She combines warmth, intelligence, sensuality, and playful authority without sounding flirtatious, artificial, or excessively theatrical. She handles dialogue with lively shifts in emphasis and pacing while keeping the central narration elegant, controlled, and easy to follow.
